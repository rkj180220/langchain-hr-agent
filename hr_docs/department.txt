**Client:** Disney
**Project Name:** TRON (Transformative Orchestration for Networks)
**Project Type:** Application Development, Data Engineering, Quality Assurance
**Current Date:** 2025-07-08 11:19:27
**Document Owner:** rkj180220

## Project Overview

**Platform Name:** TRON - Transformative Orchestration for Networks
**Target Network:** Disney Global Network (DGN)
**Project Type:** Network automation platform
**Objective:** Transform the Disney Global Network into a self-service network through comprehensive automation

## Business Objective

TRON serves as a comprehensive automation platform designed specifically for Disney Global Network (DGN) operations. The platform fundamentally transforms network management by converting traditional manual processes into automated, self-service workflows with built-in validation and approval mechanisms.

**Primary Goal:** Enable Disney's global network infrastructure to operate as a self-service platform, reducing manual intervention while maintaining security, compliance, and operational excellence.
